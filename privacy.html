Privacy Policy

This application is a private tool used only by its developer to manage the developer's own YouTube channel. It uses Google OAuth to access the developer's own YouTube account for: uploading and scheduling videos, managing video metadata, and reading the channel's analytics reports.

The application does not collect, store, sell, share or disclose any personal data of other people. The authorization token for the developer's own account is stored only on the developer's own computer. No data obtained through Google APIs is transferred to third parties or used for advertising.

Access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page (https://myaccount.google.com/permissions). This policy may be updated; the latest version is always on this page.
